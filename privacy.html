Permitly Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Permitly ("we", "the app") helps people prepare for the driver's permit test. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and sign-in details, used to create and secure your account.
  • Progress data: your practice answers, scores, mistakes and settings, so your progress is saved.
  • Advertising data: if you use the free version, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and app interaction data to show and measure ads.

Advertising

The free version of Permitly shows ads provided by Google AdMob and its partners. These partners may use your advertising ID to show personalized ads where the law allows. In regions that require consent (such as the EU, UK and some US states), we ask for your choice and respect it. You can change your choice any time in Profile → Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Premium users do not see ads. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

How we use information

To run the app, save your progress, show ads, prevent abuse and improve the app. We do not sell your account information.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers that run the app (such as authentication and database hosting) and with advertising partners described above.

Children and teens

Permitly is intended for users 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and deletion

Changes
